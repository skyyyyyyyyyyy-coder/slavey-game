# Meadow Valley Simulator 🌾

A relaxing first-person countryside farming and exploration simulator set in an open green valley surrounded by snowy mountain peaks.

---

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action | Key / Input | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Walk** | <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> | Smooth, responsive movement |
| **Look** | <kbd>Mouse</kbd> | Pointer lock free-look with clamped vertical pitch |
| **Jog / Run** | <kbd>Shift</kbd> | Jogging with stamina meter |
| **Jump** | <kbd>Space</kbd> | Physics jump with Coyote Time and Jump Buffering |
| **Crouch** | <kbd>C</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> | Smooth crouching for close inspection |
| **Harvest / Pick** | <kbd>E</kbd> or <kbd>Left Click</kbd> | Pick ripe apples from trees, harvest carrots, pumpkins, and wheat |
| **Backpack** | <kbd>B</kbd> or <kbd>Tab</kbd> | Open/close full Backpack inventory modal |
| **Look Closer** | <kbd>Right Click</kbd> | Scenic optical zoom |
| **Settings / Menu** | <kbd>ESC</kbd> | Adjust sensitivity, ambient volume, head bob, invert Y |

## 🌄 Key Features & Changes

1. **Dynamic Player Shadow**:
   - The player character (equipped with explorer clothes, straw hat, and a leather backpack) casts a real-time dynamic shadow on the ground that moves, turns, and scales with your movement and crouch!
2. **Backpack & Inventory System**:
   - Quick hotbar at the bottom of the HUD displaying your counts for:
     - 🍎 **Fresh Apples**
     - 🥕 **Farm Carrots**
     - 🎃 **Country Pumpkins**
     - 🌾 **Golden Wheat**
   - Press <kbd>B</kbd> or <kbd>Tab</kbd> to inspect the full Backpack dialog.
3. **Fruit-Growing Trees**:
   - Orchard trees now grow sweet apples.
   - Approach a tree and press <kbd>E</kbd> to pick fruit directly into your backpack.
   - Fruits naturally regrow over time through a visual growth cycle (budding → swelling → ripening).
4. **Farming & Fields**:
   - The entire area is now a plain, open countryside meadow with tilled farm plots:
     - **Carrot Patch**: Harvest fresh carrots that regrow from the soil.
     - **Pumpkin Patch**: Harvest fat golden pumpkins.
     - **Wheat Field**: Harvest golden wheat bundles.
     - **Central Stone Well**: A rustic village well in the center of the plots.
5. **No Barriers on Walkways**:
   - Removed all fences and obstacles along the trail for completely unobstructed walking.
   - Removed all rocks and boulders across the plain.
6. **Perimeter Mountain Barriers**:
   - The valley is encircled on all four sides by massive, rugged mountain ranges (28m–42m tall) with snowy peaks that keep players inside the valley and provide a mountain skyline.
